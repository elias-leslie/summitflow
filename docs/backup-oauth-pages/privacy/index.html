Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy covers the SummitFlow Backups OAuth app and this website. The app is for its owner’s use; it has no public accounts.

Google Drive access and use

The app requests Google Drive drive.file access. It creates, reads, updates, and may delete files it creates or that the account owner opens with it, solely to store and recover backups. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files.

Storage and protection

Backup content is encrypted before upload to Google Drive. The OAuth token stays in private local configuration. This website does not collect tokens, backup files, or Google credentials.

Sharing and website data

Encrypted backup files are stored in Google Drive. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or model training, or share it with other users. Cloudflare serves this static website and receives ordinary web requests. The site has no analytics scripts or forms.

Retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner’s Drive until the owner or configured retention removes them. Revoking access stops future use of the API but does not delete stored files. The owner can delete those files in Drive and revoke access in Google Account settings.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
